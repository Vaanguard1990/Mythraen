# Game Design Document

## High concept

**Mythraen: Fractured Souls** is a third-person action RPG about restoring broken souls in a decaying fantasy region. The player explores interconnected spaces, fights corrupted creatures, helps local factions, and makes choices that affect relationships and quests.

## Player fantasy

Become a wandering soul-bearer: read the scars left in the world, master a responsive weapon style, and decide which people and places deserve restoration.

## Pillars

1. **Exploration with meaning** — every route should reveal lore, resources, an encounter, or a relationship.
2. **Deliberate action combat** — readable enemy intent, player commitment, and rewarding positioning.
3. **Human-scale consequences** — quests change NPC dialogue, local conditions, and available opportunities.
4. **Data-driven growth** — content creators can add items, abilities, dialogue, and quests without modifying gameplay code.

## Build 0.1: Brumavia

The first vertical slice contains a compact village and misty paths, a third-person player, conversable NPCs, collectibles, saves, and environmental story beats.

## Core loop

Explore → observe or speak → fight or interact → obtain information/resources → improve readiness → open new routes and choices.
